Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 November 2026

Operator and contact

JARVIS Social Engine is an independent personal beta project operated by Future Wealth. Privacy requests: future.wealth.gbt@gmail.com.

Data and purpose

With account authorization, the local application may retrieve account identifiers, profile information, follower counts, video statistics and calendar events from connected services. TikTok access is being tested. Data is used for account identification, performance reporting and content planning.

Storage and sharing

The tested Google connector stores local statistics and calendar snapshots. OAuth credentials are used to authorize API requests. The planned TikTok connector is intended to store data locally; its implementation and retention settings must be confirmed before use. This informational website contains no application login, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages and connected platforms may process technical data under their own privacy policies.

Control and retention

Access is based on account authorization. Local snapshots may remain until removed by the operator. You can revoke access through the connected platform and request deletion of locally held data using the contact above. Applicable privacy rights include access, correction, deletion and objection where provided by law.

Changes

This policy will be updated before expanding access or introducing new data processing.
